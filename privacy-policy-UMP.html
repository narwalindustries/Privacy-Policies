Privacy Policy for UMP (Universal Media Player)

Effective Date: November 20, 2026 Developer: Narwal Software Solutions

🛡️ Privacy Summary: UMP is an offline-first local media player. We do not track, collect, store, transmit, or sell your personal data, media files, or viewing history. Your files stay strictly on your device.

1. Introduction

Welcome to UMP (Universal Media Player), developed and published by Narwal Software Solutions. We deeply respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy outlines how UMP handles user information, device access permissions, and data security.

By installing and using UMP, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

UMP is fundamentally designed as a privacy-first, offline-capable local media player:

  • No Personal Data Collection: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, contact lists, device identifiers (IMEI, Android ID), or browsing history.
  • No Media Uploading or Remote Tracking: Your local audio files, video files, playback positions, playlists, and folder structures remain strictly on your physical device. We never upload, inspect, scan, or transmit your media to any remote server or cloud service.
  • No Third-Party Analytics or Trackers: UMP contains zero third-party advertising SDKs, behavioral analytics trackers, or user profiling tools.
  • No Account Required: You do not need an account, sign-in, or registration to use UMP.

3. Permissions Requested & Why They Are Needed

To provide local media playback, background audio, and lockscreen controls, UMP requests the following Android permissions:

Permission Purpose & Usage
READ_MEDIA_VIDEO / READ_MEDIA_AUDIO Required on Android 13+ to discover and play local videos and audio tracks stored on your device.
READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE Used on Android 12 and below to index and access user-selected media folders and perform file operations (such as renaming or deleting a file when explicitly instructed by you).
MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE Optional access to allow indexing and playback from custom directories across external storage, matching the behavior expected of standalone local media players.
FOREGROUND_SERVICE & FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK Required by Android to keep music and audio playback running smoothly when the app is in the background or the screen is turned off.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Required on Android 13+ to display notification playback controls (play, pause, next, previous, seek) and media artwork in the notification shade.
WAKE_LOCK Prevents your device screen from turning off while you are actively watching a video.
INTERNET Permitted only for user-initiated network streams (e.g. streaming a remote media URL you explicitly provide) or opening external support links. No telemetry, user data, or analytics are ever transmitted over the network.

All permissions are used strictly to deliver media player functionality and can be revoked at any time via your device's Android System Settings.

4. Third-Party Services & Links

UMP does not integrate third-party advertising or analytics networks. If you choose to contact developer support via email or open external links (e.g., viewing our GitHub repository or Google Play Store listing), those third-party services (such as your chosen email client, GitHub, or Google Play) are governed by their respective privacy policies.

5. Children's Privacy

UMP does not address or target anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children or any users.

6. Security

Since UMP operates locally and does not collect or store user data on external servers, your data remains safely stored on your device subject to Android's built-in sandbox and security features.
