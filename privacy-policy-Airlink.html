Privacy Policy — AirLink

Developed by Narwal Software Solutions • Effective Date: November 2026
🔒 Core Privacy Principle
AirLink is a standalone, offline utility for direct file transfers between your own devices. We do not operate servers, collect zero data, and require no internet access. We have provided this application as a local tool — we have no connection to it once installed on your device.

1. Zero Data Collection

AirLink does not collect, record, track, transmit, or monetize any user information:

  • No Personal Information: No names, email addresses, phone numbers, accounts, or logins required.
  • No File or Content Tracking: The files, photos, videos, and documents you transfer remain strictly on your local hardware and are never uploaded or inspected.
  • No Analytics or Ads: Contains zero third-party analytics (no telemetry, no tracking SDKs) and zero advertising frameworks.

2. 100% Offline Peer-to-Peer Operation

All file transfers execute strictly device-to-device across your local environment:

  • Local Network / Hotspot: Files are transferred directly over your local Wi-Fi, mobile hotspot, or Wi-Fi Direct. An active internet connection is never required.
  • Proximity Pairing: Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) and QR codes are used only for local device discovery and socket exchange between paired hardware.

3. Device Permissions & Usage

AirLink requests only the minimum OS permissions essential for offline file operations:

  • Storage Access: Used solely to read the files you deliberately select to send and to save incoming files into your chosen download directory.
  • Local Network / Wi-Fi State: Used to bind local transfer sockets and manage direct device-to-device data streaming.
  • Nearby Devices / Bluetooth: Used to detect and pair nearby devices. On Android, Bluetooth discovery permissions are categorized by the OS under nearby devices/location; AirLink never reads or tracks your physical GPS location.

4. Local Channel Security

Direct socket transfers between paired devices are protected with TLS and AES-256 channel encryption to ensure that file streams cannot be intercepted by other devices on the same local network.

5. Third Parties & Data Retention

Because Narwal Software Solutions maintains no backend databases or relay infrastructure, there is zero data retained or shared with any third party. You maintain full ownership and control to delete any received files from your local storage at any time.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR)

AirLink is completely safe for all audiences, including children under 13. Because the application collects no personal information whatsoever, it fully adheres to COPPA, GDPR, and app store family policies.
